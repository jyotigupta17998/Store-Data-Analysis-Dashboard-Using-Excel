# Vrinda Store – Annual Sales Analysis Report (2024)
**Prepared by:** Jyoti Gupta  
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel (Power Query, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers)

---

## 1. Project Overview
Vrinda Store’s 2024 sales data was analyzed to identify customer behavior patterns, high-performing regions, and effective sales channels. The objective is to inform targeted strategies to increase revenue in 2025.

---

## 2. Dataset
- File: `data/raw/Vrinda_Store_Raw_Data.xlsx` (25,000+ records)  
- Processed workbook: `data/cleaned/Vrinda_Store_Cleaned_Data.xlsx` or `dashboard/Vrinda_Store_Analysis_Dashboard.xlsx`

**Key fields:** Order Date, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Age Group, State, Channel, Category, Amount, Order Status

---

## 3. Objective
Answer business questions:
- Monthly Sales vs Orders trends
- Top revenue months and states
- Gender & age group purchase behavior
- Channel performance and category insights
- Order fulfillment rates

---

## 4. Methodology
- Data cleaning via **Power Query** (standardized columns, fixed formats, removed duplicates)
- Feature engineering (Month, Year, Age_Group)
- Pivot tables + Pivot charts to compute KPIs
- Interactive dashboard built with slicers & timeline

---

## 5. Key Insights (summary)
- **Women** contribute ~64% of total sales.
- **Adults (30–49 yrs)** drive nearly **50%** of purchases.
- Top states: **Maharashtra**, **Karnataka**, **Uttar Pradesh**.
- Top channels: **Amazon (35%)**, **Flipkart (22%)**, **Myntra (23%)** — three channels ~80% of orders.
- **Delivered** orders > 90%.

---

## 6. Recommendations
1. Target marketing to women aged **30–49**.  
2. Increase ad spend and offers in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh.  
3. Run channel-specific promotions on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra.  
4. Promote best-selling categories via bundles.  
5. Investigate returns/cancellations by category and channel to reduce wastage.
